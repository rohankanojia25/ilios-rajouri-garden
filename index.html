The Story of ilios

West Delhi's own slice of the Greek islands, since 2023

In Greek, ilios means the sun. We built this sanctuary for those who crave the golden light, whitewashed arches, and breezy serenity of Santorini without boarding a flight.

What started in 2023 along the bustling Ring Road in Rajouri Garden quickly captured hearts across the capital. Between soaring ceilings and hand-textured plaster arches, ilios reimagined West Delhi nightlife: combining heartfelt multi-cuisine dining, craft mixology, and soulful acoustic performances under one golden roof.

Following the love received in Rajouri Garden, the ilios journey expanded to Greater Kailash and Gurugram (M3M Broadway, Sector 71)—bringing consistent hospitality and Aegean charm to Delhi NCR.
