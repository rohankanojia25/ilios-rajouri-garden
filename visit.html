Why ilios

West Delhi's own slice of the Greek islands, since 2023

In Greek, ilios means the sun. We built this space with white walls, pink shutters, wicker pods and warm lights for people who want the breezy serenity of Santorini without boarding a flight.

Upstairs, wicker cocoon pods and a ring daybed; inside, a lantern-lit room and the bar. Opened on Ring Road in Rajouri Garden in 2023, ilios quickly became a favorite for weekend friend dinners, birthday milestones and live Sufi nights.

As love for the space grew, ilios expanded to Greater Kailash in South Delhi and Gurugram at M3M Broadway.
